## The Timeline Problem You've Identified

Within FY 2023-24 three things are happening **simultaneously:**

```
Apr 2023 ──────────────────────────────── Mar 2024

1. Old customers' AMC contracts expiring → Renewed or Churned
2. New machines being sold every month  → New customers entering
3. New AMC contracts being signed       → From new machine sales
```

If we don't separate these cleanly — the data becomes a mess where new customers inflate your renewal base and distort your churn rate.

---

## The Clean Solution — Three Distinct Populations

**Population 1: Existing Customers With AMC Due**
- Who: 600 customers acquired BEFORE Apr 2023
- What: Their AMC from prior year expires sometime in FY 2023-24
- Analysis: Renewed or Churned → **Churn Rate**
- Records in fact_amc_contracts: ~3,000 (600 customers × avg 5 machines)

**Population 2: New Customers Acquired In FY 2023-24**
- Who: 200 customers who bought their first machine Apr 2023 - Mar 2024
- What: They sign a fresh AMC at point of purchase
- Analysis: Did they sign AMC or not → **AMC Attachment Rate**
- Records in fact_machine_sales: ~1,000 new machines sold
- Records in fact_amc_contracts: ~800 new AMC contracts signed

**Population 3: Existing Customers Buying Additional Machines**
- Who: Some of the 600 existing customers expanding their fleet
- What: They buy more machines AND have existing AMC due for renewal
- Analysis: Both attachment AND renewal apply
- This is a small subset — maybe 10-15% of existing customers

---

## How fact_amc_contracts Handles All Three

Add one column — `contract_type` — to distinguish:

```
contract_type = 'Renewal'    → Prior year AMC being renewed or churned
contract_type = 'New'        → Fresh AMC from new machine purchase
```

Your churn analysis then becomes surgically clean:

```sql
-- Churn Rate: Existing customers only, renewal contracts only
SELECT 
    amc_status,
    COUNT(*) AS contracts,
    ROUND(COUNT(*) * 100.0 / SUM(COUNT(*)) OVER(), 2) AS pct
FROM fact_amc_contracts
WHERE contract_type = 'Renewal'
GROUP BY amc_status;
```

And AMC attachment rate:

```sql
-- Attachment Rate: New contracts from new machine sales
SELECT
    COUNT(DISTINCT ac.sale_id) AS machines_with_amc,
    COUNT(DISTINCT ms.sale_id) AS total_machines_sold,
    ROUND(COUNT(DISTINCT ac.sale_id) * 100.0 / 
    COUNT(DISTINCT ms.sale_id), 2) AS attachment_rate
FROM fact_machine_sales ms
LEFT JOIN fact_amc_contracts ac 
    ON ms.sale_id = ac.sale_id 
    AND ac.contract_type = 'New'
WHERE YEAR(ms.sale_date) = 2023 
    OR (YEAR(ms.sale_date) = 2024 AND MONTH(ms.sale_date) <= 3);
```
